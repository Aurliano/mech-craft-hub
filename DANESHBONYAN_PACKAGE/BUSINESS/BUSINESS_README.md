## 📊 خلاصه برنامه تجاری

### مدل کسب‌وکار MechCraft Hub

#### مدل کلی: B2B2C Marketplace
```yaml
Business Model:
  - Type: Two-Sided Marketplace
  - Primary Value Proposition:
    - For Customers: Access to qualified contractors
    - For Contractors: Steady project pipeline
    - For Platform: Commission from successful matches
  
  Key Stakeholders:
    - Customers: Engineering companies and individuals
    - Contractors: Freelance engineers and engineering firms
    - Platform: MechCraft Hub (facilitator and enabler)
```

#### جریان ارزش (Value Flow):
1. Customer posts project requirements
2. AI algorithm matches qualified contractors
3. Contractors submit proposals
4. Customer selects best proposal
5. Project execution with quality monitoring
6. Payment processing and commission collection
7. Feedback and rating system

### مدل درآمد

#### منابع درآمد اصلی:
1. **کمیسیون از پروژه‌ها** (Primary Revenue - 70%)
   - Small Projects (<$1,000): 15% commission
   - Medium Projects ($1,000-$5,000): 12% commission
   - Large Projects ($5,000-$15,000): 10% commission
   - Enterprise Projects (>$15,000): 8% commission

2. **اشتراک ماهانه** (Subscription Revenue - 20%)
   - Basic Plan: $50/month
   - Professional Plan: $100/month
   - Enterprise Plan: $200/month

3. **خدمات اضافی** (Additional Services - 10%)
   - Technical Consulting: $150/hour
   - Training Programs: $500 per course
   - Premium Support: $200/month
   - Custom Development: $10,000+ per project
